# Golden Hour: phone checklist

Walk this on a real iPhone in the TestFlight build, on a normal connection, before a release is called
done. The automated tests use stand-in services; this is the check against the real ones. If a step fails,
open Places, the gear, Status, and send a screenshot of that page.

## First run

1. Open the app. A sample town shows an answer in words ("Go tonight", "Maybe tonight" or "Not tonight").
2. Tap **Add my place**, then **Use my location**. Allow location when asked.
3. Your town replaces the sample. Within about ten seconds you can read: go or not, the sunset time, and (on a
   Go or Maybe) a place with **Plan this evening**.

## Tonight

4. The line under the times says "Updated" with a time. Tap **Refresh**: it updates.
5. Under the answer, a place appears, or "Looking for places…" and then a place within about 45 seconds.
   If it says "Couldn't load places", tap **Try again**.
6. Tap the place. A sheet opens over the page with "Know before you go". Swipe from the left edge: it closes.
7. Tap **Why**. The score, the five times and the tiles appear. Tap it again to hide them.
8. Tap an evening in "The evenings ahead". Its sheet opens; swipe sideways for the next evening; tap Done.
9. Under the week, pick **Another date** a few months ahead: exact times, and "Colour can't be forecast yet".

## A plan

10. Tap **Plan this evening**. The plan opens with the place filled in. There is no Save button.
11. Tap **Share**. The share sheet opens at once, with a picture. Send it to yourself in Messages.
12. Open the link you sent on the phone: the same answer, the times and "Start back by".
13. Back in the plan, change **Be there by**, then tap **Send update**: the message starts "Changed: now…".
14. Tap **Add to calendar**: Calendar's new-event sheet opens with the times and a 30-minute alert.
15. Tap **Text a friend where I'll be**: Messages opens with the place and times filled in. Cancel it.
16. Tap **Back**, then **Plans** in the bar: the plan is there.
17. In Plans tap **New plan**, **Somewhere else**, and search for a venue by name. It pins the venue.

## Places and locations

18. Tap **Places** in the bar and add a second town. On Today, swipe sideways anywhere on the page: it moves
    between the two, and the bar shows "1 of 2".
19. On Today tap **All places near …**. Turn on **Parking** and **Toilets**. Either places with both are
    listed, or it says none are listed here.

## Coming back

20. Close the app. Turn on aeroplane mode. Open it: the last forecast shows, marked "Offline".
21. Turn aeroplane mode off and open the app the next morning: it refreshes, and asks "How was it?" once.

## The three journeys (time each)

- **Alone after work:** from opening the app to knowing go or not, the time and the place. Target: 10 seconds.
- **Four friends:** from opening the app to a plan sent to a group chat. Target: 3 taps.
- **Mum with two children:** from opening the app to a place with parking and toilets, or being told none
  is listed. Target: 2 taps from "All places".
